# Synthetic maritime overlay data

Navy / maritime feeds for the Trojan mothership disagreement demo
(SDTH issue #116). These files are generated by
[`generate_maritime_overlay.py`](../generate_maritime_overlay.py) with
`seed=42`. Coordinates are synthetic WGS84 chosen for a coherent Singapore
Strait / Jurong map story, not a model of real naval sensors.

## Narrative core

Wall-clock window (same as Scenario 1):

```text
14:30:00 through 15:00:00
```

- **AIS**: `HAPPY TUG 8`, MMSI `563098710`, ~6.1 kt near
  `(1.2148, 103.6648)`.
- **Coastal radar**: track `RDR-NAVY-024`, ~120 kt UAS inbound toward Jurong.
- **GLINT SAR**: hull ~78.5 × 14.2 m, aft-deck metallic structure near the
  mothership.
- **Story EW**: Air and Army LOBs pointing at the mothership (triangulation
  demos).

The intentional contradiction is AIS routine traffic versus coastal radar UAS
kinematics on the same patch of water.

## Files in this directory

```text
synthetic_maritime_data/
  scenario_config.json
  ais.json
  coastal_radar.json
  glint_sar.json
  pois.json
  ew_story_overlay.json
  all_overlay_events.json
```

- `scenario_config.json` — seed, time window, mothership and UAS track
  parameters.
- `ais.json` — 7 AIS reports (5-minute updates).
- `coastal_radar.json` — 16 coastal-radar UAS updates (2-minute cadence).
- `glint_sar.json` — 1 GLINT SAR candidate on the mothership hull.
- `pois.json` — POI-01 Jurong CNI (buffer / offshore no-fire) plus military
  stubs.
- `ew_story_overlay.json` — Air Force and Army EW bearings toward the
  mothership.
- `all_overlay_events.json` — chronological merge of AIS, radar, and GLINT
  only (no EW LOBs).

Raw AIS / radar / GLINT records keep their native schemas. POIs and EW story
overlays are supporting products for C2 interlock and LOB demos, not fused
tracks.

## Regenerate

From the repository root:

```text
python generate_maritime_overlay.py
```

Writes only under `synthetic_maritime_data/`. Downstream C2 consumers should
prefer the canonical stream under [`../exports/`](../exports/) produced by
`generate_canonical_stream.py`.
